01 / ABOUT

I care about the details people usually don't notice.

I'm a frontend developer who enjoys the space between design and engineering — where a layout decision, a transition timing, or a single line of copy changes how a product feels to use. I build with React and TypeScript, and I spend as much time refining interaction details as I do writing logic.

Most of what I build starts as a small, useful idea: a cleaner way to browse something, a form that feels less like a form. I like shipping things people actually open more than once.
